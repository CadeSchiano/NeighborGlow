Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

This policy explains how NeighborGlow handles information when you browse the Halloween activity map, create an account, list a home, participate in community updates, or use Candy Finder+.

Who operates NeighborGlow

NeighborGlow is operated by Cade Schiano, acting as an individual developer. For privacy questions or requests, contact sideforgeapps@gmail.com.

Accounts are optional

You can browse the map without creating an account. An account is required to create or manage a house listing, submit a candy-availability confirmation, report a listing, purchase Candy Finder+, restore a purchase, or delete an account.

When you create an account, Supabase Authentication processes your email address and password credentials. NeighborGlow uses your Supabase user ID and private profile record to operate the account. The app keeps sign-in session information and an onboarding-completion preference locally on your device so it can keep you signed in and remember whether you completed onboarding. NeighborGlow does not display your email address or account ID on the public map.

Age eligibility and privacy questions

Anonymous map browsing is available at any age. Account registration is for users 13 years or older. Candy confirmations and reports require an account and are for users 13 years or older. Publishing a residential house listing is for users 18 years or older who are authorized to publish that location. These are eligibility requirements; NeighborGlow does not currently verify users' ages.

If you are a parent or guardian with a privacy concern about information connected to a child, contact sideforgeapps@gmail.com. Please do not include passwords, access tokens, or other sensitive credentials in your message.

Location and map information

NeighborGlow may request foreground location permission when you choose to center the map around you. You can skip permission and browse another area. The app uses the location returned by your device for that map action; it does not send your live location or location history to NeighborGlow’s database.

Homeowners choose a map point for a house listing. If a listing is active, its selected map coordinates, availability, hours, and homeowner-selected attributes may be visible to map users. This is intentionally public map information. Do not publish a location unless you have permission to do so.

The Glow is a coarse neighborhood visualization based on participating-house data. It does not show individual visitors’ locations or location history.

House listings and community contributions

For a house listing, NeighborGlow stores the selected coordinates, availability status, trick-or-treat hours, selected attributes, and candy inventory. Public house details can include availability, hours, selected attributes, and aggregate recent-confirmation information. Exact candy details and full-size filters are limited to eligible Candy Finder+ users.

When signed-in community members confirm candy availability, NeighborGlow stores the selected status, their account ID, and timestamps. Individual confirmer identities are not displayed publicly. When members report a listing, NeighborGlow stores the report reason, optional text they provide, their account ID, and timestamps for moderation. Reports are not public.

Purchases and Candy Finder+

Candy Finder+ is a one-time seasonal purchase, not a subscription. Apple processes iOS purchases. RevenueCat helps NeighborGlow manage purchase identity, restoration, and entitlement status. NeighborGlow stores a server-verified entitlement for the signed-in account; it does not rely on a client-side purchase claim alone.

A qualifying purchase may be restored to a newly created NeighborGlow account using the same Apple Account. The 2026 Halloween Pass does not extend beyond its advertised seasonal end date. Apple’s purchase and refund history is separate from NeighborGlow account records.

Service providers and data sharing

NeighborGlow uses Supabase for authentication, database services, and secure server functions; RevenueCat for purchase-related services; Apple for iOS payment processing; and device/platform map services to render maps. Those providers process information needed to provide their services under their own terms and privacy practices.

This legal website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical information needed to serve the site under its own privacy practices. NeighborGlow does not include third-party advertising analytics, behavioral tracking, or a web cookie banner in this legal website. The app’s public-map data is visible to other map users as described above.

Security and retention

NeighborGlow uses access controls and server-side checks intended to limit access to account and purchase data. No system can guarantee absolute security.

NeighborGlow has not adopted a separate fixed retention period for account, listing, confirmation, or report data in this MVP. Data remains while needed to provide the service or until removed through the account/listing flows described here. We do not promise immediate erasure from backups or independent third-party systems. A final retention policy, including any legal-preservation obligations, requires ongoing business and legal review.

Deleting your account

You can initiate deletion from the authenticated Profile screen. Deletion removes the NeighborGlow account and associated account-linked data, including the user’s profile, owned listing, associated candy inventory, account-linked confirmations and reports, Candy Finder+ entitlement, and associated RevenueCat webhook records. Deleting an owned house also removes confirmations and reports attached to that listing.

NeighborGlow requests deletion of connected RevenueCat customer data before deleting the Supabase account. RevenueCat processes customer deletion asynchronously, so that external deletion request may not finish immediately. Deleting a NeighborGlow account does not automatically refund a purchase, cancel an Apple purchase, or delete Apple’s independent transaction history.

Privacy requests, changes, and contact

You can use the in-app account-deletion option to remove an account and associated app data as described above. For privacy questions or other requests, contact sideforgeapps@gmail.com. We may update this policy as NeighborGlow changes. Material updates will be posted here with an updated effective date.
